Owner: Susank Shakya

<aside>
📁

**`docs/vendor-portal/`** — the seller / beauty-advisor app: guided consented consultations, product-to-need mapping, and recommendation review.

</aside>

`vendor-portal` (Next.js 15.5.18 / React 19.2.6, `:3004`, `@nuvia/vendor-portal`) is the in-store application used by sellers and beauty advisors to run consented consultations, curate the product mappings that feed scoring, and review recommendation quality before presenting it to a customer. Like the other frontends it is a thin, role-gated client that talks only to `backend-engine`, holds no provider or storage credentials, and is governed by Policy authorization and audit logging ([ADR Pack](https://app.notion.com/p/ADR-Pack-36ff29d2a6b18108958af03b19f13a6e?pvs=21) ADR 0012). Seller consultation is the **Phase 4 — Seller consultation foundation** of the roadmap.

# Principles

- Sellers act only on behalf of consenting customers.
- Every action is authorized by Policy and audit-logged (ADR 0012).
- No provider/storage credentials in the client; private media only via short-lived signed URLs.
