**Description**

The three-sided ecosystem assumes manufacturers will provide verified data and want anonymized demand insights, while customers will consent to sharing.

**Root cause**

The plan assumes marketplace network effects before proving single-shop value.

### Impact

- **Business:** Building brand portals and sampling flows too early wastes effort.
- **Technical:** Adds consent, aggregation, anonymization, brand tenancy, product ownership, sampling campaigns, and analytics complexity.
- **Customer:** Customers may see brand sharing as surveillance, not value.
- **Risk if unresolved:** Scope creep and trust erosion.

**Recommended resolution**

Defer brand/manufacturer features until shops show repeat usage. Start with retailer-only analytics: mapped product performance and consultation conversion.
