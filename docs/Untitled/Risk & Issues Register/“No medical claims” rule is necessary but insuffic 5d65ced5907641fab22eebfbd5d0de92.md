**Description**

Docs correctly prohibit diagnosis, cures, clinical treatment claims, and medical framing. But recommendations still involve skin type, concerns, adverse reactions, ingredients, sensitivities, and patch-test language.

**Root cause**

Cosmetic guidance can easily drift into quasi-medical advice.

### Impact

- **Business:** Regulatory and liability exposure.
- **Technical:** Requires copy governance, blocked terms, review workflow, and product-claim constraints.
- **Customer:** Users may misinterpret recommendations as dermatological advice.
- **Risk if unresolved:** Legal/reputation risk.

**Recommended resolution**

Create a retail-safe language policy enforced in templates and recommendation output. Use only neutral language: “may suit,” “seller review recommended,” “not medical advice.”
