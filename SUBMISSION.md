# Nuvia Beauty

## Short Write-Up

Nuvia Beauty is a multivendor AI beauty marketplace built on the existing Zyro Fashion commerce base and enhanced with Perfect Corp virtual try-on APIs. Shoppers can browse vendor products, open product pages with vendor identity intact, upload a photo, and preview how items may look before purchasing. The experience is photo-based and focused on retail value: better product discovery, more confident shopping, and fewer returns.

## Perfect Corp APIs Used

- AI Clothes Virtual Try-On
- AI Watch Virtual Try-On
- AI Bag Virtual Try-On
- AI Shoes Virtual Try-On

## Consumer and Retail Value

- Helps shoppers visualize products before purchase
- Preserves multivendor marketplace browsing and vendor identity
- Improves product confidence for fashion items
- Supports clothing and accessory try-on in one shopping flow
