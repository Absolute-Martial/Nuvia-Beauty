# Goals and Non-Goals

Define what this project aims to achieve and what is explicitly out of scope. This helps align the team and prevents scope creep.

---

## 🎯 Goals

### Business Goals
* **Goal 1**: (e.g. Provide a virtual-try-on experience for users to purchase fashion products)
* **Goal 2**: (e.g. Enable multi-tenant vendor stores under a single beauty storefront portal)

### Technical Goals
* **Goal 1**: (e.g. Standardize release engineering across all backend and frontend applications)
* **Goal 2**: (e.g. Establish clean APIs and clear boundary definitions for components)

---

## 🛑 Non-Goals

The following items are explicitly **out of scope** for this project at this stage:
* **Non-Goal 1**: (e.g. Building native Android/iOS storefront applications)
* **Non-Goal 2**: (e.g. Handling direct multi-currency tax jurisdictions dynamically)
