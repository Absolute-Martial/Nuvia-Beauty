# Glossary

Dictionary of business domain terms, technical terminology, and abbreviations.

| Term / Abbreviation | Definition | Context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **VTO** | Virtual Try-On. The ability for customers to virtually test items (makeup, apparel, accessories) using their camera or uploaded photos. | Storefront & YouCam |
| **ADR** | Architecture Decision Record. A short text document capturing an architectural decision, its context, and consequences. | Engineering |
| **Dokploy** | An open-source, self-hosted Platform-as-a-Service (PaaS) used for application deployment. | DevOps |
| **DoD** | Definition of Done. A list of criteria that must be met before a work item is considered complete. | Engineering |
