# Vendor Portal Routes

## Vendor routes

- `/login`
- dashboard index
- product management
- vendor shop oversight
- vendor staff management
- support and message surfaces where enabled
- vendor content views where enabled

## Hidden or restricted MVP areas

- General settings
- Payment settings
- SEO settings
- Company information
- FAQ editor
- Terms editor
- Shipping
- Withdrawals
- Shop transfer request
- Layout and page management

## Rule

Vendor routes are private, must not be linked from the public storefront, and
must not remain the long-term home for platform admin workflows.
